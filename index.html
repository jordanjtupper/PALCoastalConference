Workshop overview

Built for Louisiana’s port community.

This annual PAL workshop brings port leaders, public officials, and industry partners together at Port Fourchon for coastal learning, discussion, meals, fishing, and on-site tours.

01

On the water

Participants can request a fishing spot, with inland or offshore preference, subject to available space.

02

Inside the port

Port Fourchon presentations and tours are scheduled for both fishing and non-fishing attendees.

03

Time together

The program includes a welcome steak supper, cocktail hour, seafood boil, breakfast, and a closing fried seafood feast.
