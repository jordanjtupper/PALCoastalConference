Workshop Overview

Connect. Learn. Experience the Coast.

The PAL Coastal Workshop brings together Louisiana port professionals, industry partners, and invited guests for an immersive look at Port Fourchon and the people who keep Louisiana's maritime economy moving.

Attendees will gather for meals, fishing, port presentations and tours, the PAL Board Meeting, and informal conversations throughout the three-day workshop.
